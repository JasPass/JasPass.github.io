Topological susceptibility

The topological susceptibility, i.e., the fluctuations of the topological charge at high temperature where perturbative QCD can be applied, is studied. The path integral is expanded around field configurations with non-zero topological charge, e.i. instantons. The contribution from so-called soft modes (\( k \ll T \)) is computed, bringing perturbative results for the topological susceptibility in agreement with lattice simulations.
